Genesis

Chapter 1

1In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth.
2The earth was formless and empty, darkness covered the surface of the deep, and the Spirit of God was hovering over the waters.
3Then God said, "Let there be light," and there was light.
4God saw that the light was good, and God separated the light from the darkness.
5God called the light "day," and the darkness He called "night." There was evening and there was morning—the first day.
