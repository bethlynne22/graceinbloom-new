Today's Journey

In the beginning, there was God. He spoke, and light appeared. Darkness gave way, the heavens stretched wide, and the earth became a home filled with life. Genesis 1 reminds us that creation is not accidental or forgotten—God lovingly gives it order, purpose, and blessing.

What Happens

God creates the heavens and the earth, brings light from darkness, orders the world, fills it with life, and makes people in His image.

Look For

Light, sky, land, seas, plants, sun, moon, stars, birds, sea creatures, animals, and the repeated phrase that creation is good.

Remember

Genesis 1 teaches that creation is not random or forgotten. God speaks, orders, blesses, and calls His creation very good.

Genesis 1:1 “In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth.”
